Welcome to the National Park website. We hope you enjoy the National Parks as much as we do. We have links to our favorite hikes, campgrounds, lodging, restuarants, stores, etc. The National Park Service also provides a wage range of webcams throughout the United States Park System. These live webcams allow you to get up close to wildlife without disturbing them.

For all the national parks, please visit US National Parks (national-parks.us)

There are 63 national parks, 35 containing webcams. A few of the parks have live streams that we were embed within the website. The links to the sites are provided on the individual web page. Also, due to inclement weather a few of the NPS webcams do not refresh.

The NPS provides a free downloadable app that allows you to explore more than 400 national parks across the country. The app features interactive maps, tours of park places, accessibility information, and more. You can also download content from entire parks for offline use, share virtual postcards with your friends, and find out the latest news, alerts, and events for the parks you’re interested in. The link is at the bottom of the page.

We are also added information on our favorite hikes, restaurants, stores and campgrounds within the different parks. Below are helpful external links to various sites for making reservations and planning for your stay.

It is worth noting that 12 United States National Parks are UNESCO World Hertige Sites. They are Carlsbad Caverns National Park, Everglades National Park, Glacier Bay National Park, Grand Canyon National Park, Great Smoky Mountains National Park, Hawaii Volcanoes National Park, Mammoth Cave National Park, Mesa Verde National Park, Olympic National Park, Redwood National Park, parts of Wrangell-St. Elias, Yellowstone National Park, and Yosemite National Park

In addition, Isle Royale National Park and Rocky Mountain National Park are UNESCO International Biosphere Reserve due to it's diverse plant and animal life.

Some of the National Parks have only one or two webcams, while others are just amazing. These are the ones that we consider the must see: Channel Island, Crater Lake, Glacier Bay, Glacier, Grand Canyon, Hawaii Volcanos, Isle Royale, Lassen, Mount Rainer, Olympic, Rocky Mountain, Yellowstone and Yosemite.

Even though these National Parks have limited webcams, they have incredible views: Acadia, Black Canyon of the Gunnison, Grand Tetons, Wrangell-St. Elias, and Zion.

Short History of the National Park

The National Park System is a collection of more than 400 areas that are managed by the National Park Service (NPS), a federal bureau in the Department of the Interior. The NPS was created by President Woodrow Wilson on August 25, 1916, when he signed the"Organic Act"into law. The National Park System has since expanded to include 428 individual units covering more than 85 million acres in all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and US territories. These units are often referred to as"parks"and are managed by the NPS to conserve the nation's natural and cultural heritage for the benefit of current and future generations.

The National Park System is divided into 20 separate categories, including national parks, national preserves, national monuments, national recreation areas, national seashores, national scenic trails, and national historic sites, among others. The parks are managed together as an administrative unit within the National Park Service. The majority of the related areas are not units of the National Park Service and are managed by other government agencies or non-government organizations and landowners.

The National Park System is an important part of American history and culture. It attempts to explain America's history, interpret its culture, represents and preserve its varied ecosystems, and provide for the recreation of its 250 million people. The parks have been justifiably called"the crown jewels"of America and"the best idea America ever had".

The National Park System is home to some of the most iconic and beautiful landscapes in the world. Some of the most popular parks include Yellowstone National Park, Yosemite National Park, Grand Canyon National Park, and Zion National Park. These parks offer visitors a chance to experience the beauty of nature, learn about the history of the United States, and participate in a variety of recreational activities.

Yellowstone National Park, located primarily in Wyoming, was the first national park in the United States and is widely considered to be the first national park in the world. It is home to a variety of wildlife, including grizzly bears, wolves, and herds of bison and elk. The park also features the famous Old Faithful geyser, which erupts every 90 minutes or so.

Yosemite National Park, located in California's Sierra Nevada mountains, is known for its stunning granite cliffs, waterfalls, and giant sequoia trees. The park is a popular destination for rock climbers, hikers, and photographers. The park's Half Dome and El Capitan are two of the most famous rock formations in the world.

The Grand Canyon, located in Arizona, is one of the most famous natural landmarks in the world. The canyon is 277 miles long, up to 18 miles wide, and over a mile deep. Visitors can hike, raft, or take a helicopter tour of the canyon.

Zion National Park, located in Utah, is known for its towering sandstone cliffs and narrow canyons. The park is a popular destination for hikers and rock climbers. The park's most famous hike is the Angels Landing Trail, which offers stunning views of the park from the top of a 1,500-foot cliff.

In conclusion, the National Park System is a collection of more than 400 areas that are managed by the National Park Service. These parks are an important part of American history and culture and offer visitors a chance to experience the beauty of nature, learn about the history of the United States, and participate in a variety of recreational activities. Some of the most popular parks include Yellowstone National Park, Yosemite National Park, Grand Canyon National Park, and Zion National Park. Each park has its own unique features and attractions that make it a must-visit destination for anyone who loves the great outdoors.
